# Privacy Policy for MineColonies Auth

_Last updated: August 2026_

At auth.minecolonies.com, we keep things as simple and transparent as possible. We respect your privacy and collect only the absolute minimum information required to operate our service.

## Information We Collect

Account Identifiers: When you authenticate with us (e.g., via Discord, Patreon, or Minecraft/Pex), we receive basic public profile information and unique IDs to verify your identity and support status.

## How We Use Your Data

Reward Unlocks: We use your account details solely to check your eligible support tiers and unlock your earned in-game or community rewards within MineColonies.

Essential Functionality: Maintaining your active login session while you use the authentication portal.

## What We Don't Do

No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties or advertisers under any circumstances.

No Tracking or Profiling: We do not track your activity across other websites, compile behavioral profiles, or use third-party advertising cookies or analytics trackers.

No Surplus Usage: Your data is strictly used for reward verification and account management,nothing else.

## Data Retention

We only store your authorization tokens and associated platform IDs for as long as necessary to maintain your linked account status and grant your rewards.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or your account data, please reach out to the MineColonies team via our official Discord server or community channels.
